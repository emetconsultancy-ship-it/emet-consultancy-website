EMET CONSULTANCY SERVICE CO.,LTD. — WEBSITE STARTER

Files:
- index.html: Complete responsive one-page website. No build tools or installation required.

Preview:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

Before publishing:
1. In index.html, replace YOUR-COMPANY-EMAIL@example.com in the JavaScript with your real business email.
2. Replace the email, phone, and office-address placeholders in the Contact section.
3. Review the service descriptions and remove or edit any services that your company does not provide.
4. Add your real logo if you have one (the current EM mark is a text-based placeholder).

Publishing:
- Upload index.html to your hosting provider's public web folder (often called public_html), or deploy it with a static website host.
- The contact form opens the visitor's email app; it is not a server-backed form. A server or form service is needed for direct website submissions.
